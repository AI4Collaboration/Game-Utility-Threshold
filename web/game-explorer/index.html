WELCOME TO THE TIDE

Two players. Two choices.
A world of consequences.

  1. Pick a game. Each game gives the players a different reason to work together, compete, or coordinate.
  2. Read the table. Rows are Player 1’s moves; columns are Player 2’s. Each square shows both players’ points.
  3. Change one thing. Move the slider and watch the table and calculated average points update. All other payoff settings return to the preset when you choose a different control.
  4. Try it yourself. In “Play a round,” the other player’s move is drawn from the displayed chance before you choose. It is not a live AI or another person.

“Neither gains by changing alone” means that, with the other player’s move held fixed, neither can earn more by switching. It does not mean the result is best for both.

The Paper 1 and repository scenario presets use different payoff settings. Practice rounds are saved only in this page and never enter the research dataset.
