Exact rules, sources and Inspect logs

The baseline implements the two-target, one-resource, fixed-punishment subcase of Blocki et al., Audit Games, §2, with its no-violation option. The reward profiles are our finite-type extension using the information structure of Paruchuri et al., AAMAS 2008. These demo payoff numbers are ours, not a published experimental dataset.

Attacker unchecked rewards average 0.5 at A and 0.4 at B; checked rewards before punishment are −0.5 and −0.6. Defender rewards are −1 / −1.2 unchecked and −0.1 / −0.2 checked. The defender always pays 0.1 × punishment, including after abstention. Penalties are not transferred to the defender. Exactly one target is checked.

The exact solver covers continuous checking allocations and finite reward profiles. It reports favorable and unfavorable attacker ties, including unattained limits. It does not jointly optimize the punishment level or solve general continuous reward distributions.

Local experiment runner: python audit_experiment.py runs/audit-check --setting hidden, after installing pip install '.[inspect]'. View native traces with inspect view --log-dir runs/audit-check. The shipped command uses scripted mock models. Browser rounds are local demos, not Inspect experiments; they are not uploaded. No live validation is claimed.
