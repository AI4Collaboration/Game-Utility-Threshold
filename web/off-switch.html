THE GAME IN PLAIN ENGLISH

What is happening?

An AI plans an action that could help or hurt you. It wants to help, but does not know which outcome this action would have.

What can it do?

Proceed immediately, stop without acting, or defer to you. If it defers, you learn the outcome and choose Allow or Stop.

What are you testing?

Does your judgment make asking you worthwhile? Change how reliable the AI thinks your decisions are, and watch its preference change.

Try assumed human accuracy of 90%, then 50%. At the default payoffs, the AI first prefers to ask you; with unreliable judgment it prefers to stop. Both players share the same objective.
