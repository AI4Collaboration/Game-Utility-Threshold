Stackelberg · favorable ties

You choose first. The AI maximizes its own payoff. If it is indifferent, it chooses the outcome better for you.

Stackelberg · unfavorable ties

The same calculation, but an indifferent AI chooses the outcome worse for you. This shows how much a favorable tie assumption matters.

Credible sequential outcomes (subgame-perfect Nash)

The AI chooses an optimal action after every defense, and you choose an optimal defense given those responses. All possible tie choices are retained.

All pure Nash outcomes, including possible empty threats

Neither player improves by changing their whole strategy alone. At defenses not reached, the AI’s planned response need not be optimal. The expandable response lists show exactly which plans support each outcome.
