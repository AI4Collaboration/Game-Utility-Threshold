  • Each cell shows its mean and available / eligible observations. N/A is missing or inapplicable, never zero. Averages pool observations, not cell means.
  • Model views use each participant's own action, regret, utility, confidence and belief where available. Joint outcomes are attributed to both participants; self-play contributes two dependent role appearances. Treatment and cross-play views average the two roles for individual metrics.
  • “Cooperation” and “competition” name first and second actions. Lockdown and quarantine are legitimate alternatives; Nash equilibrium is not automatically safe. Utility scales vary across games, and external subsidies enter payoffs.
  • Binding commitments restrict available actions; their equilibrium results are partly mechanical. One observation per factorial cell does not support within-cell uncertainty estimates.
  • Threshold derivations, risk-dependent boundaries and observed switching diagnostics are in figures 22–26. Observed brackets are descriptive constraints, not confidence intervals. Four-game empirical switching curves require additional parameter-sweep experiments.
