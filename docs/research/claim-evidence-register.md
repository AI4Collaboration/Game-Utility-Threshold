# Paper 1 claim and evidence register

**Status snapshot: 7 November 2026.** This register states what the present
repository can support and what remains before publication. It is a claim
checklist, not a percentage-complete score. The latest project validation
reports **315 passing tests, one skipped test and 340 passing subtests**.
The skipped check requires the unavailable Lean/Lake toolchain. Passing
software tests does not establish empirical findings or replace a proof.
No paid model calls have been made for the new prospective studies.

The [protocol](paper-one-protocol.md) defines the experiment and completion
gates. The [literature review](literature-review.md) and
[13-entry bibliography](references.bib) explain which established methods
are reused and where their assumptions limit our claims.

The next evidence-producing steps are pilot qualification, a written pilot
decision memo, a separately frozen confirmation study and final analysis.
Mathematical review and the Lean build can proceed independently. Historical
observations must keep their original exploratory status; new documents do
not retrospectively change their design.

**No universal safety claim is supported.** Numerical reward maximization,
coordination, modeled attack prevention and real-world human safety are
different outcomes. A result must name its payoff model, information
assumptions, tested model/settings and evidence type. No result here proves
that an arbitrary AI is safe, that uncertainty always produces deference,
or that an untested deployment will obey a benchmark threshold.
